1. Overview & Data Collection

DF Heat Labs LLC built T'ai Chi for All as a free educational and community wellness utility. This service is provided at no cost and is designed with a strict privacy-first architecture.

We do not collect, process, store, transmit, or monetize any personal information or sensitive user data.

2. Third-Party Services & Tracking

The application operates entirely on your local device:

  • No third-party analytics, diagnostic telemetry, or behavioral tracking SDKs are embedded.
  • No third-party advertising networks are utilized.
  • No account registration, user profiling, or social media logins are required.

3. Device Permissions & Local Storage

Any settings or progress indicators configured by the user are stored strictly on the local device file system and are never synchronized to external cloud databases. If media or instructional video files are accessed, they are rendered locally without logging playback data.

4. Children’s Privacy

This application does not address anyone under the age of 13 specifically, nor do we knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.
