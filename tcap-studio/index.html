Privacy Policy

TCAP Studio | The Talitha Cumi Awakening Project

Effective Date: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how The Talitha Cumi Awakening Project (TCAP) and its technical engineering partner, DF Heat Labs, LLC ("we," "us," or "our"), collect, use, and protect information when you access or use the TCAP Studio mobile and web applications (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

To provide a functional psychoeducational resource hub and consultation platform, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: When you register for an account, we collect your name, email address, password, and professional role (or personal-use status).
  • Commission & Intake Data: Information submitted via custom curriculum or workshop requests, including organizational focus, audience demographics (general age ranges or population context), and desired learning outcomes. Identifiable personal data of students, patients, or clients is strictly prohibited and not collected.
  • Consultation Details: Contact and scheduling details entered when booking focused consultations or strategy sessions.
  • Community Board Activity: Text and reflections posted publicly within the Launch Member Board.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information solely to:

  • Authenticate users and gate access to curricula, video teachings, and toolkits.
  • Process custom curriculum commissions and coordinate consultation appointments.
  • Facilitate community discussions on the Launch Member Board.
  • Distribute project updates and TCAP newsletters (subject to your explicit opt-in preference).

3. Cloud Infrastructure & Third-Party Services

We utilize secure third-party service providers to power the App:

  • Firebase (Google LLC): Used for secure user authentication, database management (Firestore), and asset storage.
  • Calendar & Scheduling Providers: Utilized to synchronize booked consultations with administrative availability.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties or data brokers for advertising purposes.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA)

TCAP Studio is designed for adult educators, mental wellness professionals, organizational leaders, and individual adult learners. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information directly from children under the age of 13. Curricula referencing youth or childhood trauma are intended solely for adult practitioners and facilitators.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

In compliance with Apple App Store and Google Play policies, users have the right to request deletion of their account and all associated data at any time. You may request account deletion directly within the App settings or by contacting our administration.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes by posting the updated policy within the App and updating the "Effective Date" at the top.
