DF Heat Labs Product Privacy Notice

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 4, 2026

Application: Samiyrah Weaver Counseling (Mobile App)

Developer & Publisher: DF Heat Labs

This Privacy Policy describes how DF Heat Labs ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in relation to the Samiyrah Weaver Counseling mobile application (the "App"), developed for Samiyrah Weaver, LPC.

1. Information Collection and Use

The App is designed solely as a client quick-reference directory and informational guide. We do not collect, store, transmit, track, or share any personal data, sensitive health information, device identifiers, or usage analytics.

  • No Account Required: You can use the App without registering an account or providing any personal details.
  • No Data Tracking: The App does not track your location, browsing history, or interaction data.
  • No Analytics or Ads: The App does not contain third-party advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or telemetry analytics tools.

2. App Permissions & Device Functionality

The App provides interactive buttons for client convenience (e.g., calling the practice line, sending an email, or opening professional profiles). When you tap these buttons, the App uses standard system intent handlers to launch your device’s default applications:

  • Phone Calls (tel:): Opens your device's native Phone dialer app.
  • Email (mailto:): Opens your default Email client.
  • Web Links (https://): Opens external web pages (such as Headway, Psychology Today, or local crisis resources) in your default Web browser.

No phone numbers, email contents, or web activity are recorded or processed by the App.

3. Third-Party Services & External Links

The App contains links to external websites and resources (including Headway, Psychology Today, Healthy Minds Philly, NAMI, and Calm). These external services are operated independently and have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party websites or services you visit.

4. Emergency & Crisis Services Notice

The App provides buttons to quickly dial emergency support services, including the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline and 911 Emergency Services. Dialing these numbers connects you directly to official national or local emergency dispatchers outside of the App.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience (adults age 18 and older seeking professional counseling reference information). The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date".
