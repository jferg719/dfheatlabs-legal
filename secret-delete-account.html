Account & Data Deletion Request

App: I Have a Secret (formerly Sin City Confessions)
Developer: DF Heat Labs, LLC

Option 1: Delete Your Account Inside the App (Immediate)

  1. Open I Have a Secret on your Android or iOS device.
  2. Navigate to your Profile / Character Vault settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account & Data and confirm your selection.

In-app deletion immediately removes your anonymous account credentials, custom handle, district code, and associated profile data.

Option 2: Request Deletion via Web / Email

If you no longer have the app installed, you may request full account and data deletion by emailing support@dfheatlabs.com with the subject line "IHAS Account Deletion Request" and your anonymous handle or district code.

Verified requests are processed within 7 business days.

What Data Is Deleted vs. Retained

  • Deleted Permanently: Account authentication identifiers, custom persona handles, mask/aura preferences, and saved profile metadata.
  • Content Removal: Associated text and pitch-shifted voice confessions linked to your account are removed from active feeds upon deletion.
  • Retained for Legal/Billing Compliance: Anonymized transaction records required by Google Play, Apple App Store, or Amazon Appstore for tax and financial auditing may be retained as required by law.
